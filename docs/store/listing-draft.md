# Soft Strange Drawing — listing draft

Short description: A quiet mixed reality studio for tracing sharks and nature, one gentle line at a time.

Description:

Make a little space to draw. Choose an outline, place it over your paper, and lock it where it feels right. Soft Strange Drawing keeps the reference visible in mixed reality while you draw with your own pencil or pen.

Explore 60 cleaned AI illustrations and 60 stylized model subjects with matching side and top views, including shark and nature studies. Adjust the lines from solid to dotted, choose a color, and soften the opacity. An independent grid helps you line things up without moving your reference. Bring your own PNG or JPEG through the headset's file picker.

Use your hands to point and pinch, with two hands to resize, or use controller triggers for the menus and grips to position the reference. Gentle original sounds and animated miniature models make the studio feel welcoming; sound and decorative motion can be turned down in Studio.

The bundled drawing library works offline. Your imported images stay on your headset. Physical paper and a drawing tool are needed for the intended activity; this app does not track your pen or record your physical drawing.
